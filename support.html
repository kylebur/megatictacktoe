Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a game in iMessage?

Open the Messages app on your iPhone or iPad, tap into any conversation with a friend or family member, tap the App Drawer (+) icon below the text bar, select MegaTicTacToe, and tap New Game to send the first move.

What if my opponent doesn't have MegaTicTacToe installed?

When you send a move to a friend who doesn't have MegaTicTacToe yet, Messages will display the interactive game bubble with a preview. Tapping the bubble will automatically direct them to download MegaTicTacToe from the App Store so they can immediately reply with their move.

How does the turn mechanism and board choice work?

MegaTicTacToe is played on an 81-cell board consisting of a 3×3 grid of 9 mini boards. Whichever cell you pick inside a mini board determines which mini board your opponent must play in on their turn. For instance, clicking the bottom-left square of any mini board forces your opponent to play somewhere inside the bottom-left mini board.

What happens if I am sent to a mini board that is already won or full?

This triggers the Free Choice rule! If your opponent's move directs you to a board that has already been won or is tied/filled, you are free to place your symbol in any available square anywhere on the entire board.

Can I play solo against the computer?

Yes! In the MegaTicTacToe app header, switch to Solo AI mode. You can practice strategies against the built-in MegaAI bot offline without needing an active internet connection.

Does MegaTicTacToe collect my data or read my messages?

No. MegaTicTacToe does not collect, track, or share any personal data or messages. Moves are encoded strictly into Apple's official iMessage session URLs directly between participants. Read our full Privacy Policy for complete details.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please email us at kyle.burchesky@mac.com with your iOS version and a brief description of the issue or feature suggestion. We love hearing from our players!
