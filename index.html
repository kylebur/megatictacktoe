Game Guide

How Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe Works

Easy to learn in two minutes, but filled with boundless tactical opportunities.

1

The 3×3 Grid of 3×3 Boards

The game consists of 9 mini Tic-Tac-Toe boards arranged in a 3×3 master grid (81 total cells). Player X makes the first move anywhere on the board.

2

Dictating Your Opponent's Board

Whichever cell you choose in a mini board determines which mini board your opponent must play in next. For example, playing in the top-right corner of a mini board forces your opponent to play in the top-right mini board!

3

Winning a Mini Board

Get three of your marks (X or O) in a row inside any mini board to win and claim that entire board for your symbol.

4

Free Choice Rule

If you are sent to a mini board that has already been won or is tied/full, you are granted free choice to play in any unoccupied cell across the entire board.

5

Claiming the Master Victory

Win three mini boards in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on the master grid to win the entire MegaTicTacToe match!
