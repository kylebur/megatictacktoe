Privacy Policy for MegaTicTacToe

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. MegaTicTacToe does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, telemetry, or gameplay data.

1. Information Collection and Storage

MegaTicTacToe operates entirely on your device and within your Apple iMessage conversations. We do not operate external game servers, maintain database accounts, track user analytics, or collect device identifiers (such as IDFA or vendor IDs).

2. iMessage Game Transmissions

Game turns and board states are encoded directly inside standard Apple iMessage message URLs sent directly between you and your conversation participants using Apple's end-to-end encrypted Messages infrastructure. The developer never intercepts, reads, or receives your messages or game states.

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

MegaTicTacToe contains no third-party advertisements, tracking SDKs, data brokers, or analytics frameworks. There are zero background network requests made to third-party endpoints.

4. Children's Online Privacy

Because MegaTicTacToe does not collect any personal data whatsoever from any users, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy standards.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out directly via our Support Page or by email at kyle.burchesky@mac.com.
